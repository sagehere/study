# Validated Pilot Patterns

## Procedural — trial quotient adjustment
Diagnostic: judge whether a remainder can still form a complete group. Require a reason before a fast path. Repair remainder constraints or approximate-vs-original verification separately. Formalize only after evidence. Independent evidence: decide whether a trial quotient must go down. Transfer: new divisor/dividend with no step-by-step prompt.

## Concept-representation — perimeter and area
Start with tasks that reveal the measured object: walking/fencing an edge versus covering a surface. Use a same-number conflict (e.g. perimeter and area both numerically 16 with different units) to force quantity reasoning. Derive formulas from side sums and tile rows rather than displaying formulas first.

## Quantity-relation — unit price, quantity, total
Establish “per one” before formula language. Derive total as repeated equal unit prices. Then switch the unknown to unit price and quantity. Repair direct division of a composite total by asking whether the entire total belongs to the requested item. Transfer across unit granularity (box → item).

## Boundary-concept — rounding
Locate a number between endpoints before teaching the digit mnemonic. Make the exact midpoint/threshold a separate case. Include a magnitude sanity check. Reverse the operation into an interval and explicitly test which boundary is included. Transfer to a new target interval.

## Evidence pattern
A robust package normally includes: diagnostic evidence, misconception-triggered repair, formalization unlock, independent pass, transfer pass, and review scheduling after strong help. Correct answer + wrong reason should not be treated as full understanding.
